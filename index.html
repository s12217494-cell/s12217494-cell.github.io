Volume I · of IV

An-Najah LibraryAI-powered book discovery for the university library

Role
Lead developer
When
2025 – 2026
Problem
Keyword-only catalogue; reader reviews unused
Result
Search by meaning, per-aspect sentiment, a grounded chatbot

The university catalogue only supported keyword search, and reader reviews sat there unused. I scraped the catalogue (2,197 books in 8 subjects, Arabic and English), enriched it with Goodreads metadata and reviews, and built four AI features on top of it:

  1. Semantic search. Multilingual 384-dimension sentence embeddings stored in ChromaDB, so a query finds books by meaning, in either language.
  2. Recommendations. A hybrid KNN recommender over the same vectors and reader favourites.
  3. Aspect-based sentiment. Two fine-tuned Arabic transformers: MARBERT finds what a review talks about (plot, style, ending…) and CAMeLBERT-MSA decides how the reader feels about it. Reviews in other languages are translated to Arabic first.
  4. RAG librarian. A chatbot that retrieves matching catalogue entries and passes them to an LLM, so its answers are grounded in books the library actually holds.

    Palestine Job Market AnalysisWhat employers actually ask for, measured from 16,206 job postings

    Role
    Sole developer
    When
    2026
    Problem
    No clear picture of the skills Palestinian employers want
    Result
    16,206 clean postings, skill demand by region, CV matching

    The platform collects every posting on jobs.ps since January 2023. A Cloudflare-safe scraper fetches the pages and a cleaning pipeline removes duplicates and normalizes locations and dates. An LLM then extracts the required skills from each posting, and bge-m3 embeddings let job seekers upload a CV and see their matching jobs, their skill gaps, and a plan to close them. The front end is an Arabic, right-to-left Next.js site backed by FastAPI.
